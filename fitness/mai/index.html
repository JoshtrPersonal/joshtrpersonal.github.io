What’s Actually Going On

1) It’s more body composition than body weight

At this height and weight, the issue is not “huge weight.” The bigger factor is where fat is stored and how much muscle shape is underneath. Right now the body reads softer rather than shaped.

2) The lower stomach/waist area is the main gap

The biggest difference between the current look and the goal look is: less lower stomach fullness, less waist/lower-back softness, and a cleaner waist-to-hip transition.

3) Posture matters

A relaxed core and posture can make the stomach project more from the side. Core, glutes, back work, and standing posture can improve this a lot.

4) The goal photos are real, but also posed

Low-rise clothing, twisting, angles, sucking in, lighting, and styling all help. So the goal is realistic to move toward — but those exact photos are also aesthetic presentation.

Honest Summary

The blunt version:

To get closer to the look in goal photo 2 and goal photo 4, the body likely needs:
  • a smaller waist,
  • a flatter lower stomach,
  • less fat around the lower back / sides,
  • more glute and hip shape,
  • better posture and tighter core control.
This is most likely a 6-month cut/recomp, not a “go gym casually and hope” situation. It also does not need starvation. The best result comes from losing fat slowly while building shape.

Timeline

Phase 1 — Weeks 1 to 4

Goal: reduce bloating, create routine, start fat loss
  • Calories: 1,750–1,900/day
  • Protein: 90–110g/day
  • Steps: 7k–10k/day
  • Gym: 3 sessions/week
  • Expected result: less bloating, slightly tighter waist, better posture, 1–2kg down

Phase 2 — Weeks 5 to 12

Goal: visible waist and stomach change
  • Calories: 1,600–1,800/day
  • Protein: 100g/day
  • Steps: 8k–10k/day
  • Keep increasing gym weights or reps slowly
  • Expected result: 3–5kg down total, smaller waist, reduced belly bump, cleaner back/side line

Phase 3 — Months 4 to 6

Goal: shaping phase — closest to goal photo 2 and 4
  • Calories: 1,700–1,900/day or maintenance if already much leaner
  • Protein stays high: 100g/day
  • Gym: 3–4 sessions/week
  • Expected result: flatter stomach, better hip/waist contrast, better glute shape, closer to aesthetic target

Weekly Training Plan

Day 1 — Glutes + Legs

  • Hip thrust — 3 sets of 8–12
  • Leg press — 3 sets of 10–12
  • Romanian deadlift — 3 sets of 8–10
  • Cable kickbacks — 3 sets of 12–15
  • Hip abduction machine — 3 sets of 15–20
  • Dead bugs — 3 sets

Day 2 — Back + Shoulders + Core

  • Lat pulldown — 3 sets of 8–12
  • Seated row — 3 sets of 8–12
  • Shoulder press — 2–3 sets of 8–12
  • Lateral raises — 3 sets of 12–15
  • Face pulls — 3 sets of 12–15
  • Side plank — 3 sets each side

Day 3 — Glutes + Legs + Abs

  • Bulgarian split squat — 3 sets each leg
  • Glute bridge / hip thrust — 3 sets of 8–12
  • Hamstring curl — 3 sets of 10–12
  • Leg press or goblet squat — 3 sets of 10–12
  • Cable crunch — 3 sets of 10–15
  • Reverse crunch — 3 sets of 10–15

Why this split works: the goal look is not just “smaller.” It’s smaller waist + flatter stomach + better glute/hip shape. That means fat loss and shape-building at the same time.

Food Structure

Daily Targets

  • Calories: usually 1,600–1,900/day depending on phase
  • Protein: 90–110g/day
  • Water: 1.5–2.5L/day
  • Steps: 8k–10k/day
  • Sleep: aim for 7–9 hours

Simple Meal Rule

Every meal should include:

  • 1 protein source
  • 1 carb source
  • fruit or veg
  • a small amount of fat

Example meals: Greek yoghurt and fruit, eggs on toast, chicken rice bowl, salmon and rice, stir fry with protein, wraps, noodles with chicken/tofu/prawns.
