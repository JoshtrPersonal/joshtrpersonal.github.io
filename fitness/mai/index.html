Honest understanding

What The Goal Actually Means

The goal body is not just about weighing less. It is about body composition. The main gap is likely lower stomach and waist softness, lower back or side softness, posture, and not yet having enough glute or hip shape to create the stronger waist-to-hip contrast.

The current body does not need extreme dieting. The aim is not to become tiny. The aim is to lose fat slowly while building shape. Stomach projection can come from lower belly fat, bloating, a relaxed core, and posture. The goal photos are also helped by posing, low-rise clothing, lighting, angles, and styling.

Timeline

The 3–6 Month Plan

The first month should feel like control returning. The next two months should show visible change. Months four to six are where the goal shape has the best chance to come through.

Phase 1: Weeks 1–4

Goal: reduce bloating, build routine, begin fat loss
  • Calories: 1,750–1,900/day
  • Protein: 90–110g/day
  • Steps: 7k–10k/day
  • Gym: 3x/week
  • Expected result: less bloating, slightly tighter waist, better posture, 1–2kg down

Phase 2: Weeks 5–12

Goal: visible waist and stomach change
  • Calories: 1,600–1,800/day
  • Protein: around 100g/day
  • Steps: 8k–10k/day
  • Training: keep progressing weights or reps
  • Expected result: 3–5kg down total, smaller waist, reduced belly bump, cleaner back and side line

Phase 3: Months 4–6

Goal: shape phase, closer to the goal photos
  • Calories: 1,700–1,900/day or maintenance if already leaner
  • Protein: around 100g/day
  • Gym: 3–4x/week
  • Expected result: flatter stomach, better waist-to-hip contrast, stronger glute shape, closer to the body-goal look

Weekly workout

Three Training Days

Day 1 — Glutes + Legs

  • Hip thrust: 3 sets of 8–12
  • Leg press: 3 sets of 10–12
  • Romanian deadlift: 3 sets of 8–10
  • Cable kickbacks: 3 sets of 12–15
  • Hip abduction: 3 sets of 15–20
  • Dead bugs: 3 sets

Day 2 — Back + Shoulders + Core

  • Lat pulldown: 3 sets of 8–12
  • Seated row: 3 sets of 8–12
  • Shoulder press: 2–3 sets of 8–12
  • Lateral raises: 3 sets of 12–15
  • Face pulls: 3 sets of 12–15
  • Side plank: 3 sets each side

Day 3 — Glutes + Legs + Abs

  • Bulgarian split squat: 3 sets each leg
  • Hip thrust or glute bridge: 3 sets of 8–12
  • Hamstring curl: 3 sets of 10–12
  • Leg press or goblet squat: 3 sets of 10–12
  • Cable crunch: 3 sets of 10–15
  • Reverse crunch: 3 sets of 10–15

This plan works because the goal is not just smaller. It is smaller waist, flatter stomach, better posture, and more glute/hip shape.

Food structure

Simple, Not Obsessive

Daily Targets

  • Calories: 1,600–1,900 depending on the phase
  • Protein: 90–110g/day
  • Water: 1.5–2.5L/day
  • Steps: 8k–10k/day
  • Sleep: 7–9 hours where possible

Meal Rule

Every meal should have protein, a carb, fruit or veg, and a small fat source.

  • Greek yoghurt, fruit, granola
  • Eggs and toast
  • Chicken rice bowl
  • Salmon rice bowl
  • Chicken or tofu noodles
  • Stir fry with protein
  • Wraps
  • Protein yoghurt
  • Fruit
  • Protein shake

Reality check

What To Expect

1 month

Slightly tighter, less bloated, better posture. Not final body yet.

3 months

Proper visible change. Smaller waist, reduced lower stomach fullness, cleaner side and back line.

6 months

Best chance at getting close to the goal body, especially if training and food are consistent.

12 months

Best long-term version, because glutes and shape take time to build.

Final message

Supportive, But Real

This is achievable if it is followed properly. The goal is not to become as small as possible. The goal is to become leaner, more shaped, and more confident. If the plan is done seriously for 3–6 months, the body can look very different. If it is done casually, results will stay casual too.
